# Branch Manager Guide (1 page)

Your PIN is the branch's approval key: voids, price overrides, stock adjustments, payment verification, and count corrections all require it — and every use is logged with your name.

## Morning
- **Receive deliveries** (More → 🚚): start a draft, scan items + qty + cost as they come off the truck. Busy? Leave it as a draft and **Post** later — posting adds everything to the 2F stockroom in one tap.
- Check **🏠 Home**: yesterday auto-totaled, low-stock count, open orders.

## During the day
- **Payment review** (📦 Orders): open orders with 📎 proof attached, check the screenshot against your bank/GCash app, then **Verify payment → Paid** (your PIN). This deducts stock and books the sale automatically.
- **Voids / price overrides / adjustments:** staff will call you — enter your PIN, pick a reason. Adjustments for damage/expiry: Stock tab → tap the qty number.
- **Transfers** (More → 🔁): confirm quantities when sending (deducts your stockroom) and when receiving (adds to it). Any difference between sent and received is flagged automatically.
- **Attendance** (⏰): today's punches show selfie thumbnails; ⚠ flags mean outside geofence or missing selfie — tap review after checking.

## End of day
- **📈 Reports → Today**: total, onsite vs online, per payment method, voids, and **cash expected in drawer** — the numbers you used to compile by hand. Print or export CSV.
- **Stock count** (More → 🔢) when needed: pick floor or stockroom, scan every unit, apply corrections with your PIN.
- **Reorder** (More → 🧾): items at/below threshold with suggested order quantities — print as a PO draft.
