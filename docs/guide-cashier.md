# Cashier / Staff Guide (1 page)

**Start of shift:** Open the CMN app → tap your name → enter your PIN. Go to **⏰ Clock in** → take a live selfie (camera only) → done. If GPS says you're outside the store it still records, just flagged for the manager.

## Selling (POS tab 🛒)
1. Scan the item — it goes straight into the cart. No barcode? Type the name in the search box.
2. Suki or wholesaler? Pick them in the **customer dropdown** — their price applies automatically.
3. Tap **Charge** → choose Cash (type amount received — change is computed), GCash, or Bank.
4. Enter **your own PIN** — this records the sale under your name.
5. Tap **Print** for the receipt. Mistake? Ask the manager to **Void** (needs their PIN).

## Pulling stock from 2nd floor (More → ⬇️ Pull Down)
Before carrying anything downstairs: **scan it, set qty, Confirm.** Scanning the same barcode again adds +1. That's the whole job — under 10 seconds. Never bring stock down without scanning.

## Online orders (📦 Orders tab)
- New order from Messenger/Viber/call: **+ New order** → pick or quick-add the customer → scan/search items → Create. Target: under 1 minute.
- Tap an order card to open it. Use the **Copy reply** buttons and paste into Messenger/Viber — confirmation, payment details, and "paid na po" messages are pre-written.
- Customer sent payment screenshot → **Attach transfer screenshot** on the order. The manager verifies it.
- After packing: **Take packed photo** (required before marking packed). When the courier takes it → **Picked up**.

**End of shift:** ⏰ **Clock out** with a selfie.
