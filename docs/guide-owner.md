# Owner Guide (1 page)

Log in as owner (from any device) — you see **all 6 branches** and can switch the active branch from the dropdown in the top bar.

## Daily pulse (🏠 Home)
- Today / this week / this month across all branches, side-by-side branch comparison chart, top products this week, and any branch's 14-day trend.
- Every peso here comes from POS sales and verified online orders — nothing is hand-tallied.

## Reports (📈)
- Pick any branch + date range → sales list, totals split by channel and payment method, voids (with who/why), cash expected. Export CSV for your accountant.

## Buying (More → 🧾 Reorder)
- One screen lists every item at/below its low-stock threshold **across all branches** with a suggested order quantity (threshold × 2 − current). Print or export as a purchase-order draft.

## Control (More → ⚙️ Admin)
- **Users & PINs:** add/deactivate staff, set roles and 4-digit PINs. A staff member who leaves is deactivated, never deleted — history stays.
- **Branches:** geofence radius per branch (widen it if indoor GPS causes too many false flags).
- **Settings:** receipt header/footer, paper width (58/80mm), tax line label, and the bank/GCash details that appear in the order reply templates.
- **Audit log:** every void, adjustment, price override, payment verification, and attendance correction — filterable by person.

## What to watch weekly
- Movements ledger (any branch → More → 📒): pull-downs vs sales tells you if the floor process is being followed.
- Attendance flags: repeated out-of-geofence punches from the same person deserve a conversation.
- Transfer discrepancies: flagged automatically when received ≠ sent.
